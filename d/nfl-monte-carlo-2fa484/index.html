How it works

  1. Downloads nflverse play-by-play for this season and last season (weighted lower) and collapses it to one outcome per drive: touchdown, field goal, empty, or defensive score.
  2. Builds offense and defense rates per team, shrunk toward the league average so two games of data don't swing everything, and blends each matchup log5-style with a small home-field boost.
  3. Draws each team's possessions from a Poisson distribution around the two teams' average pace, rolls every drive, and records the margin and total 10,000 times.
  4. Fair spread = mean margin, fair total = mean combined score, plus win and cover probabilities at -3.5 and -7.5 and the chance of going over the market total.
python nfl_sim.py --season 2026 --week 3 --sims 10000 --out week3.csv
