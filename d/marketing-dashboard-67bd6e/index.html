How it runs

GoHighLevel + Meta (+ Google Ads)→daily job at 6 AM PT→your Google Sheet
  • You get an alert if a run fails, and the sheet keeps yesterday's numbers instead of writing zeros.
  • Instant-form leads and landing-page leads are matched differently. In week one I confirm which each client uses.

Funnel: ad spend to revenue

Appointments count on the day they were booked (including ones later cancelled or no-showed); show-ups on the appointment day; sales on the day the opportunity was marked won. Chips compare each step with the targets of the treatments in view.

    Attribution: lead to campaign to treatment

    1. Ads carry UTMs: on Meta utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_id={{campaign.id}}, on Google the campaign id from {campaignid}. GoHighLevel stores them on the contact's attribution.
    2. The job matches each lead by campaign id first (survives renames), then by campaign name with case and spacing ignored.
    3. The campaign's treatment comes from its name, CLIENT_TREATMENT_CHANNEL_Offer, or from a row on the Settings tab for names that don't follow it.
    4. Appointments and sales inherit the lead's campaign, so revenue lands on the campaign and treatment that produced it.

    Daily refresh

    A scheduler (cron on a small server, GitHub Actions or Cloud Run) starts the job at about 6:00 AM PT, after the last client's day has closed. It re-pulls the last 30 days every run and overwrites them, so a sale marked won three days after the consult still lands on the right day. If a source is behind, nothing is written and you get an alert.

    Self-booked vs setter-booked comes from the appointment's createdBy in GoHighLevel (a team member's user id means a setter booked it). I confirm the rule per account in week one, with a contact tag as a fallback.

    Adding a client or a treatment

    1. Add one row to Clients on the Settings tab: name, GoHighLevel location id, Meta ad account, Google Ads customer id.
    2. Add a row per treatment to Treatments & KPI targets (CPL, lead to appt, appt to sale, ROAS).
    3. Name campaigns TDW_BOTOX_FB_Intro99 style, or map odd names under Campaign overrides.

    The next morning's run creates the client's tab with one block per treatment and its campaigns grouped underneath. No code changes. Anything the job can't place is listed on the Data Quality tab until someone maps it.

    Definitions

    CPL
    Ad spend / leads
    Appointments
    Bookings made that day, self-booked plus setter-booked, including ones later cancelled or no-showed
    Lead to appt
    Appointments / leads
    Appt to sale
    Sales / appointments
    ROAS
    Revenue from won opportunities / ad spend
    Counting
    Each event counts on the day it happened in the client's timezone and keeps the campaign of the lead that produced it. Leads with no campaign are listed under Data Quality, not in CPL.
